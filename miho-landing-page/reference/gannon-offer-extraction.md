## The entry offer: AI Tools Assessment

- **Format:** 45-min discovery call → AI-assisted analysis → client-facing report → 30-min review call
- **Price:** $999
- **Gannon's own ICP:** small business owners, 2–20 employees, $500K–$5M revenue
- **Promise:** 3–7 off-the-shelf AI tools prescribed, to reclaim 5–10 hrs/week (his average client: 7)
- **Gannon's guarantee:** if the assessment can't find at least 5 hrs/week of opportunity, 100%
  refund. Worst case for the client is 45 minutes lost, not money.
- **Conversion:** 50–60% of assessment clients want paid implementation help
- **LTV:** $3K–$10K+ per assessment client

### The four delivery phases
1. **Discovery call** — recorded (AI note-taker). Pure probing, no pitching, no prescribing on
   the first call. Key question: "If you could delete any process in your business, what would
   it be?"
2. **AI analysis** — feed the transcript to Claude (or a purpose-built skill), have it research
   pain points against real tools. Human QA pass required — substitute tools appropriate to
   business size (his example: don't prescribe Salesforce to a 4-person landscaping business).
3. **Report generation** — built in a design tool (Claude Design / Gamma). Contains: executive
   summary, effort-vs-impact matrix ("quick wins" vs. "major projects"), recommended solutions
   (tool, cost, setup time, time saved), a 4-day quick-start plan, a financial-impact/ROI slide,
   next steps. Iterated 12 times toward maximum simplicity — "a confused mind doesn't buy,
   doesn't implement, doesn't upsell."
4. **Review call** — 30 min, walk through the report, three closing questions: which
   recommendation is most urgent? DIY or want help implementing? what's your timeline?

## Upsell menu (six items, sold after the assessment)

1. **Process redesign** — fix a broken process before automating it. Example price: $3,000–3,500.
2. **Simple automation build** (Zapier/Make/n8n) — cut-and-dry 1–3 step flows. Example: $1,500 one-time.
3. **Knowledge system** — custom GPT/knowledge base trained on the business's own material.
4. **Custom workflows** — Claude Skills for a proprietary process; can carry a maintenance retainer.
5. **AI Concierge (the flagship recurring offer)** — done-with-you retainer. Two 45-min calls/month
   teaching Claude Co-work and building Claude Skills, plus "unlimited" Voxer access (12-business-hour
   response — in practice almost never used, high perceived value). Priced $1,200–2,000/mo per
   client, rising with each new signup. Effective rate ~$1,000/hr. Deliberately capped client count
   (Gannon caps at 6) creates real scarcity.
6. **Full implementation** — any bundle of the above. Example: $8,000 package.

**Credit trick:** credit the $999 assessment fee toward the upsell, but mark the upsell up by
$1,000+ first — client feels like they got a discount, Gannon stays flat-to-profitable.

## Seven client-acquisition channels (Gannon's own GTM — not page copy, internal reference only)

1. Local "AI for business" meetup, hosted free in a co-working space in exchange for exposure
2. Door knocking — ask for the owner/manager directly
3. LinkedIn DMs to local business owners — probe pain, never pitch in message one
4. Free 15-minute mini-audits for personal network, as a lead magnet into the paid assessment
5. Referral partnerships with adjacent professionals (accountants, insurance agents, marketing
   agencies) — referral fee, optional co-branded workshops
6. AI "office hours" — a standing weekly walk-in slot inside a co-working space
7. Build in public — document small wins as content, compounds over months

## Proof points and credibility markers in the source material

- ~15 assessments sold in 2026; average client saves 7 hrs/week; average prescribed tool stack
  costs ~$60/mo; monthly net ROI "always four figures, sometimes five"
- AI Concierge: 5 of the first 6 people pitched said yes; $8K MRR in the first 10 days; 4 of 5
  clients hit their stated 90-day win, some in under 60 days
- Concrete client examples: an e-commerce seller's 10-hr/week Amazon ad-spend process (redesigned
  before automating); a wedding-venue Asana workflow automated for $1,500, saving 30 min/week and
  scaling with growth; a business broker's custom GPT that cut 500 buyer emails per listing to zero

## Open item for Howard: the guarantee mechanism doesn't match what's already logged

Gannon's actual guarantee is conditioned on **the diagnosis itself finding enough opportunity** —
if the assessment can't surface ≥5 hrs/week of savings, refund. It's checkable at report time,
before the client has done anything.

The guarantee Howard specified this session (`reference/decisions.md`, "Audit carries a
money-back guarantee, conditioned on follow-through, not a time window") is conditioned on **the
client following through and not getting value** — only checkable after the client acts.

These solve different objections and aren't mutually exclusive, but they're not the same
guarantee — worth a conscious choice (or combining both) at drafting time rather than blending
them by accident.
