## The problem

You've read the AI headlines. You've watched competitors post about some new workflow. You know
something in your business could run better with the right tool.

You also know you don't have time to figure out which tool, whether it's worth the setup, or
whether it just becomes one more thing that gets abandoned in a month.

That's the actual problem. Not a lack of AI. A lack of time to sort the real tools from the noise.

## The offer: the AI Audit

The AI Audit isn't a report telling you what's wrong. It's a short list of tools telling you what
to do next.

Here's how it works:

- **A 45-minute call.** We ask questions, you talk. No pitching, no jargon.
- **We turn what we hear into 3 to 7 specific tools** — picked for your business, not a generic

- **A short review call** where we walk through what to install first and what it'll save you.

**Price:** $399.

No questions asked. Worst case, you lose 45 minutes.

## Objections

**"I've heard this before."**
Every AI pitch sounds the same until it's specific to your business. This isn't "use ChatGPT
more." It's your actual bottleneck, matched to a tool that fixes it.

**"I've tried consultants before and got a binder I never opened."**
That's why the audit ends with three to seven tools, not thirty pages of theory. You'll know
what to do on day one.

**"I don't have time for this."**
You have 45 minutes. What you don't have is another year of doing this by hand.

**"Is $399 worth it?"**
If we don't find at least 5 hours a week, we refund it. That's the only guarantee we're willing
to make, because it's the only one we're sure of.
