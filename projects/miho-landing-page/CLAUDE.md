# miho-landing-page

Landing page for MiHO Partners — AI automation and consulting for small business owners.
Mike Grabham + Howard Kim. Finish line: a live page that books calls.

## What belongs here

- **Copy** — headline, sub-head, offer tiers, objection handling, CTA. Drafts in `reference/`.
- **Design direction** — the look-and-feel brief, chosen references, tokens.
- **Assets** — founder photos, generated logo, generated imagery (`assets/`).
- **Build** — the page itself, once direction is locked.

## What doesn't

- MiHO brand ground-truth (positioning, voice, ICP, proven angles) — that lives at
  `docs/miho/brand/` and is read, not duplicated here.
- Content-pipeline work — `projects/miho-content-pipeline/`.

## The design brief (locked 2026-07-28)

**Trust is the single goal.** Everything else serves it.

- **Restrained modern tech.** Not "very good accountant or law firm" — that reads boring.
  Take what's modern, clean and new, but keep it restrained.
- **Not another AI-startup site.** Rules out: near-black grounds, monospace/terminal type,
  purple-to-orange gradients, glow, grid overlays. The category is saturated; looking
  different is an asset.
- **Founder-forward.** Two named faces with real credentials is the strongest trust device
  available to a service with no brand recognition. Both headshots are in `assets/founders/`.
- **Type-, layout- and color-led.** Warm neutral grounds over white or black.
- **Motion / 3D allowed but not in the first draft**, and only where it buys trust rather
  than spectacle.
- **Must hold real content** — offer tiers, objections, proof, booking CTA. Sparse
  one-screen visual statements can't do the job.
